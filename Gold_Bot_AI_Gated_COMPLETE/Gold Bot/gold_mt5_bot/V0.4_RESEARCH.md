# v0.4 research result

Data: 50,000 broker XAUUSD M15 candles, chronological 70/30 split.

Experimental rules:

- First London-session FVG inversion and later retest
- 50-period EMA direction filter
- Entries before 13:00 America/New_York
- Maximum one strategy entry per New York day
- 2.5R take-profit
- 0.5% equity risk per trade
- Broker spread plus 0.05 price-unit slippage on each fill
- Daily risk locks enforced by the backtester

Results:

| Segment | Trades | Return | Win rate | Profit factor | Max drawdown |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development 70% | 192 | 40.79% | 43.75% | 1.6016 | 6.74% |
| Locked test 30% | 76 | 11.69% | 39.47% | 1.4487 | 6.01% |

The profitability and drawdown gates passed, but the minimum of 100
out-of-sample trades did not. Therefore demo eligibility remains false.

These results are historical simulations, not promised returns. The next
required step is more unseen history or additional forward data, followed by a
signal-only demo observation stage before any order submission.
