UNIFIED GOLD CONFLUENCE STRATEGY v1.0

Purpose
This strategy combines the strongest compatible elements from the three supplied trading transcripts into one mechanical XAUUSD system:
- Higher-timeframe market bias and fair value gaps.
- H1/M15 trend alignment and points of interest.
- Liquidity sweep followed by a confirmed market-structure shift.
- Failed-auction/order-flow confirmation when reliable futures data is available.

The system must be backtested and paper traded before demo or live execution.

============================================================
1. MARKET, TIMEFRAMES, AND SESSIONS
============================================================

Market
- XAUUSD only for the first validation version.

Timeframes
- Daily: broad directional context.
- H4: primary bias, displacement, FVG, order block, and external liquidity.
- H1: trend alignment and current dealing range.
- M15: point of interest, liquidity sweep, structure shift, entry, stop, and target.

Trading windows
- London window: 07:00-10:00 UTC.
- New York window: 13:30-16:00 UTC.
- The precise windows must be converted from UTC using broker-server time.
- Do not open a new trade outside these windows.
- Do not open new trades late Friday.

News filter
- No new entry from 30 minutes before until 30 minutes after a high-impact USD event.
- For CPI, NFP, FOMC rate decisions, or Powell/Fed press conferences, use a 60-minute buffer on each side unless testing proves a different rule.

============================================================
2. DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Swing high
- A candle whose high is greater than the highs of the two candles immediately before and after it.

Swing low
- A candle whose low is lower than the lows of the two candles immediately before and after it.

Bullish break of structure
- A candle closes above the latest confirmed swing high.

Bearish break of structure
- A candle closes below the latest confirmed swing low.

Bullish displacement
- A strong bullish candle or sequence that closes above a confirmed swing high and leaves a bullish FVG.

Bearish displacement
- A strong bearish candle or sequence that closes below a confirmed swing low and leaves a bearish FVG.

Bullish FVG
- In a three-candle sequence, candle 3 low is above candle 1 high. The gap between those prices is the FVG.

Bearish FVG
- In a three-candle sequence, candle 3 high is below candle 1 low. The gap between those prices is the FVG.

Order block
- The final opposite-colored candle before displacement breaks structure. Treat its full high-low range as a zone.

Liquidity pool
- A confirmed swing high/low, equal highs/lows, previous-day high/low, or session high/low where stops are likely concentrated.

Bullish liquidity sweep
- Price trades below a marked low/liquidity pool but an M15 candle closes back above that level.

Bearish liquidity sweep
- Price trades above a marked high/liquidity pool but an M15 candle closes back below that level.

Bullish market-structure shift
- After a bullish liquidity sweep, an M15 candle closes above the last internal lower high.

Bearish market-structure shift
- After a bearish liquidity sweep, an M15 candle closes below the last internal higher low.

============================================================
3. HIGHER-TIMEFRAME BIAS
============================================================

Bullish bias requires all of the following
1. H4 has produced a bullish break of structure with displacement.
2. The latest H4 dealing range is defined from the impulse swing low to swing high.
3. Price is at or below the 50% midpoint of that range, or is retracing into a bullish H4 FVG/order block below equilibrium.
4. H1 structure is bullish or has not closed below the H4 invalidation swing low.

Bearish bias requires all of the following
1. H4 has produced a bearish break of structure with displacement.
2. The latest H4 dealing range is defined from the impulse swing high to swing low.
3. Price is at or above the 50% midpoint of that range, or is retracing into a bearish H4 FVG/order block above equilibrium.
4. H1 structure is bearish or has not closed above the H4 invalidation swing high.

No-trade bias
- H4 direction is unclear or sideways.
- H4 and H1 structures directly conflict.
- Price is in the middle of the range with no nearby FVG/order-block confluence.
- The higher-timeframe target has already been reached.

============================================================
4. POINT OF INTEREST
============================================================

For a long setup
- Mark a bullish H4 FVG or bullish order block in discount.
- Refine it using an overlapping bullish H1 or M15 demand/FVG zone.
- A valid zone must have caused clear bullish displacement or a break of structure.
- Mark sell-side liquidity below a recent M15 low inside or immediately beneath the zone.

For a short setup
- Mark a bearish H4 FVG or bearish order block in premium.
- Refine it using an overlapping bearish H1 or M15 supply/FVG zone.
- A valid zone must have caused clear bearish displacement or a break of structure.
- Mark buy-side liquidity above a recent M15 high inside or immediately above the zone.

Do not enter merely because price touches the point of interest.

============================================================
5. LONG ENTRY RULES
============================================================

Every condition is mandatory
1. Higher-timeframe bias is bullish.
2. Current time is within an approved trading window.
3. No news restriction is active.
4. Price enters the predefined bullish point of interest.
5. Price sweeps marked sell-side liquidity.
6. The sweep candle closes back above the swept level.
7. Within the next four M15 candles, price closes above the last internal M15 lower high, confirming a bullish market-structure shift.
8. The structure-shift move leaves a bullish M15 FVG or identifiable demand/order-block zone.
9. Place a limit entry at the 50% midpoint of that new M15 FVG. If there is no FVG, use the first retest of the shift-causing order block.
10. Cancel the pending entry if it is not filled within four M15 candles, the target is reached first, or price closes below the protected sweep low.

Long stop loss
- Place the stop below the protected sweep low plus a safety buffer.
- Safety buffer = max(current spread x 1.5, 0.10 x M15 ATR(14)).

Long target
- Primary target: nearest unswept M15 swing high, session high, previous-day high, or opposing supply zone.
- The planned target must provide at least 1.5R after estimated spread and slippage.
- If the nearest target offers less than 1.5R, skip the trade.

============================================================
6. SHORT ENTRY RULES
============================================================

Every condition is mandatory
1. Higher-timeframe bias is bearish.
2. Current time is within an approved trading window.
3. No news restriction is active.
4. Price enters the predefined bearish point of interest.
5. Price sweeps marked buy-side liquidity.
6. The sweep candle closes back below the swept level.
7. Within the next four M15 candles, price closes below the last internal M15 higher low, confirming a bearish market-structure shift.
8. The structure-shift move leaves a bearish M15 FVG or identifiable supply/order-block zone.
9. Place a limit entry at the 50% midpoint of that new M15 FVG. If there is no FVG, use the first retest of the shift-causing order block.
10. Cancel the pending entry if it is not filled within four M15 candles, the target is reached first, or price closes above the protected sweep high.

Short stop loss
- Place the stop above the protected sweep high plus a safety buffer.
- Safety buffer = max(current spread x 1.5, 0.10 x M15 ATR(14)).

Short target
- Primary target: nearest unswept M15 swing low, session low, previous-day low, or opposing demand zone.
- The planned target must provide at least 1.5R after estimated spread and slippage.
- If the nearest target offers less than 1.5R, skip the trade.

============================================================
7. OPTIONAL ORDER-FLOW FILTER
============================================================

Use this only with reliable centralized futures order-flow data, such as COMEX Gold futures. Standard MT5 tick volume is not equivalent to true bid/ask footprint data.

Long confirmation
- At the sweep low, aggressive negative delta is concentrated near the candle low but cannot produce continued downside.
- The next attempt by sellers fails at a higher price.
- Buyer aggression then produces upward price progress.

Short confirmation
- At the sweep high, aggressive positive delta is concentrated near the candle high but cannot produce continued upside.
- The next attempt by buyers fails at a lower price.
- Seller aggression then produces downward price progress.

The core strategy must be backtested without this filter first. Test the order-flow filter as a separate variant.

============================================================
8. POSITION SIZE AND PORTFOLIO RISK
============================================================

Risk per trade
- Paper/demo baseline: 0.25% of current account balance.
- Maximum after successful validation: 0.50% per trade.
- Never use the transcript's higher challenge-account risk as the live default.

Position size
- Risk cash = account balance x risk percentage.
- Stop distance = absolute difference between entry and stop.
- Calculate lot size using the broker's XAUUSD tick size, tick value, volume step, minimum volume, and maximum volume.
- Round the calculated lot size downward to the broker's permitted volume step.

Limits
- Maximum one open XAUUSD trade.
- Maximum two XAUUSD entries per trading day.
- Stop opening new trades after two consecutive losses in one day.
- Daily loss limit: 1.0% of starting daily balance.
- Daily profit target: 1.0% of starting daily balance; after reaching it, open no new trades that day.
- Never add to a losing trade.
- Scaling is disabled in version 1.

============================================================
9. TRADE MANAGEMENT
============================================================

- Never remove or widen the original stop.
- Do not move the stop merely because the trade shows a small profit.
- At +1R, move the stop to breakeven only if M15 has closed beyond a new structural level in the trade direction.
- Close the full position at the predefined structural target for version 1.
- Do not use discretionary partial exits until separately tested.
- Exit early if an M15 candle closes through the protected sweep level before the target is reached.
- Close any remaining intraday position before the broker's daily rollover unless an overnight variant has been separately tested.

============================================================
10. SPREAD, EXECUTION, AND SAFETY FILTERS
============================================================

- Do not enter when spread exceeds the maximum established during backtesting.
- Do not enter if expected slippage reduces the planned reward below 1.5R.
- Do not enter if MT5 has stale quotes, the symbol is not tradable, or required history is missing.
- Evaluate signals only after an M15 candle has closed; never use incomplete candles for confirmation.
- Generate at most one signal from each closed M15 candle.
- Store every rejected setup with its rejection reason.

============================================================
12. BACKTEST AND APPROVAL PLAN
============================================================

Test variants separately
- Variant A: core rules without order-flow data.
- Variant B: core rules plus order-flow confirmation.
- Variant C: London session only.
- Variant D: New York session only.

Minimum data
- At least two years of M15 data covering different volatility regimes.
- Walk-forward split: 70% development/in-sample and 30% untouched out-of-sample.
- Include broker spread, commission, slippage, and swap where applicable.

Minimum approval gates before demo order submission
- At least 100 out-of-sample trades.
- Profit factor at least 1.20.
- Positive out-of-sample net return.
- Maximum drawdown no greater than 10%.
- No single month contributes an unreasonable share of total profit.
- Results remain acceptable after increasing modeled costs and slippage.

Forward-paper gate
- Run continuously for at least 30 paper trades after backtest approval.
- Confirm signals use only information available at the time and do not repaint.
- Compare paper entries with chart screenshots and broker prices.

Demo/live progression
- Backtest approval -> virtual paper -> MT5 demo with minimum lot -> extended demo review -> live decision.
- Live order submission remains disabled until every preceding gate passes.
