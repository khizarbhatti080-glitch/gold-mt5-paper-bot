# v0.7 Multi-Asset Portfolio Research

The bot can now discover broker symbols, export history for a configurable
watchlist, backtest every market independently, and monitor all enabled markets
at the same time. Gold, silver, Solana, and seven major FX pairs are included as
examples. Broker suffixes must be corrected before export.

Multiple simultaneous markets are supported in observation. Automated demo
orders remain locked until each instrument passes its own out-of-sample gates
and a portfolio-level exposure simulator is validated. This prevents XAUUSD's
backtest from incorrectly authorizing SOL or forex execution.
